# KIDS KARATE & JIU-JITSU CLASSES IN NEWMARKET

At Jitsu-Do, we believe a child’s martial arts journey should be built on a foundation of technical excellence, not just high-energy play. Our Kids Foundations program introduces the fundamental biomechanics of **Karate and Jiu-Jitsu** in a structured, high-standard environment. We don’t just teach kids how to kick — we teach them how to focus, how to respect the process, and how to build genuine physical capability.

## THE DOJO  
STANDARD

Discipline is not about punishment — it’s about protocol. Our students learn the bow-in culture and the importance of listening and following technical instruction from day one.

![Kids karate class at Jitsu-Do Newmarket](/images/3x-seiza-hzWGsz3r226OhdrB.jpg)

## SAFE  
BOUNDARIES

We focus on gross motor development and body awareness. By learning the structural basics of Jitsu-Do Karate, children develop balance and physical confidence that carries into every other sport.

## A LIFELONG  
FOUNDATION

The best time to build a scaffold for a disciplined life is during the formative years. The habits built here carry forward into academics, sport, and character.

![Kids jiu-jitsu at Jitsu-Do Newmarket](/images/harrison-hip-toss-eOo9x8cW5NLwYzAL.jpg)

## WHO IT  
IS FOR

Children aged 6–12 of any experience level. High-energy kids, those who need focus and structure, and complete beginners all thrive in the Kids Foundations program.
