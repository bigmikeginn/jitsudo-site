# SCHEDULE

Consistency is the only path to mastery. Our schedule is designed to accommodate the busy professional and the dedicated student. Whether you can only carve out two classes per week or want multiple days, disciplines and even private classes for maximum progress, we’ve got you covered.

## TUITION & MEMBERSHIPS

We offer three membership streams depending on a student’s investment and commitment to their martial arts education. **Elite** is our premium training experience for students who want the fastest progress. Core is the complete Jitsu-Do training experience and our most popular membership. Basic is a simple way to get started with structured training but with some limitations.

Whichever membership stream is right for you, we don’t do high-pressure sales. Every new student begins with a **Free Trial**. We want you to ensure our technical standards and dojo culture align with your goals before any financial commitment is made.
