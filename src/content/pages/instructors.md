FOUNDER & HEAD INSTRUCTOR

### 4th Dan Jitsu-Do Karate  •  BJJ Brown Belt  •  35+ Years

Shihan Michael Ginn has spent over three and a half decades pursuing a single question: what actually works? Beginning his martial arts journey in the late 1980s, he trained across disciplines with a practitioner’s eye — stripping away ceremony where it added no value, keeping what was structurally sound, and building a curriculum grounded in biomechanics, safety, and real-world effectiveness.

In 2009, he founded Jitsu-Do Karate in Newmarket, Ontario, with a clear mandate: no fluff. The result is a hybrid system built on the structural precision of **Chito-Ryu Karate** and the ground efficiency of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — a curriculum designed to produce technically proficient, pressure-tested martial artists regardless of age or athletic background.

TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

## A LABORATORY FOR  
HUMAN CAPABILITY

Shihan Ginn’s philosophy centres on the idea that martial arts is a laboratory for human capability, not a factory for trophies. Every technique taught at Jitsu-Do has been stress-tested against that standard. Students learn not just how to move, but why the movement succeeds — building a mental model that transfers under pressure.

> “Our duty is not to win — it is not to lose.”
