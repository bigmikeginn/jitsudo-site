BEFORE YOU START

## COMMON QUESTIONS

#### How long does a trial class take?

A trial class takes 30–45 minutes. You wear your own clothes and train alongside beginners. Senior students are always present to support you — you’re never on your own.

#### Am I too old? Do I need to be fit?

You’re never too old. Anyone can do this and all students are encouraged to go at their own pace. We’re about personal progress — not competing to see who’s fastest or strongest.

#### What do I need to bring?

Just yourself and comfortable clothes. No gi, no equipment, no experience required for your first visit. We’ll guide you through everything.

#### Is there a sign-up fee or contract?

No commitment required for your free trial. If you decide to join, we offer flexible membership options — no long-term contracts forcing you to stay.
