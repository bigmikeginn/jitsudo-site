# NEWMARKET’S TECHNICAL MARTIAL ARTS HUB

At **Jitsu-Do**, we treat **martial arts in Newmarket** as a laboratory for human potential, not a factory for trophies. Our methodology is the result of four decades of stripping away the ‘fluff’ of traditional arts to find the high-standard, practical core that actually works for **modern self-defense**.

We don’t just teach you how to move; we teach you why the movement succeeds. By bridging the structural precision of **Chito-Ryu Karate** with the ground-truth efficiency of **Gracie Humaita Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)**, we provide a **Newmarket-based curriculum** built on biomechanics, safety, and real-world clarity.

Whether you are an **adult professional** seeking physical longevity or a parent looking for a disciplined **youth martial arts** ‘scaffold for life’ for your child, you are training under a system designed for technical mastery and mental resilience. Our duty is not to win-it is not to lose.

## YOUTH: TECHNICAL RESILIENCE

Our youth program serves as a ‘scaffold for life’ for teens in **Newmarket**. Moving beyond basic coordination, we teach an advanced **hybrid martial arts curriculum** that fosters mental fortitude, conflict de-escalation, and personal accountability during these critical developmental years.

## **KIDS:** **FOUNDATIONAL CAPABILITY**

Build a foundation of focus and discipline. Our **Kids Karate and Jiu-Jitsu** classes in **Newmarket** emphasize gross motor skills, respect protocols, and body awareness. We teach children that mastery is earned through effort, providing a technical start to their martial arts journey.

## ADULTS: APPLIED MARTIAL SCIENCE

Designed for the professional practitioner, our adult program integrates high-standard **Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ)** and **Jitsu-Do Karate**. We prioritize technical precision and biomechanics over ego, providing a safe, elite environment for **Newmarket adults** to develop real-world capability and physical longevity.
