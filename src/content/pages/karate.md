# KARATE

JITSU-DO KARATE

## Chito-Ryu Karate — Precision Over Power

Jitsu-Do Karate is built on the **Chito-Ryu system** — a discipline developed by Dr. Tsuyoshi Chitose to integrate traditional Okinawan technique with modern biomechanical understanding. Where most styles chase power, Chito-Ryu chases alignment. The result is a striking system that works regardless of size or strength.

At Jitsu-Do, every movement is dissected through the lens of **structure and safety**. You will learn why a technique works before you are asked to drill it. That distinction is what separates serious martial arts training from repetitive motion with no understanding.

_“The goal is not to be dangerous — the goal is to be structurally sound under pressure.”_
