NEWMARKET · FOUNDED 2009

# 35+ YEARS OF TECHNICAL  
MASTERY IN NEWMARKET

Martial arts is not a commodity — it is a lineage.

**Jitsu-Do** was founded by **Shihan Michael Ginn**, a practitioner with over 35 years of dedicated experience in the evolution of combat science. After decades of training and instruction, Shihan Ginn recognized that the traditional martial arts landscape was often cluttered with fluff and outdated methods.

He founded Jitsu-Do as a **Technical Lab in Newmarket** — a space where the structural power of **Chito-Ryu Karate** and the ground-truth efficiency of **Gracie Humaita Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu** are integrated into a single, high-standard curriculum.

We don’t believe in factory martial arts. At Jitsu-Do, every movement is scrutinized through the lens of biomechanics and safety. Our mission is to provide the Newmarket community with a training environment that prioritizes the ‘how’ and ‘why’ over the ‘what.’

“This isn’t a hobby — it’s a scaffold for a more disciplined life.”

HEAD INSTRUCTOR

## SHIHAN MICHAEL GINN

4th Dan Jitsu-Do Karate  •  BJJ Brown Belt  •  35+ Years

Shihan Ginn spent over three decades pursuing a single question: what actually works? Beginning in the late 1980s, he trained across disciplines with a practitioner’s eye — keeping what was structurally sound, discarding what wasn’t, and building a curriculum grounded in biomechanics, safety, and real-world effectiveness.

In 2009, he founded Jitsu-Do Karate in Newmarket with a clear mandate: no fluff. The result is a hybrid system built on the structural precision of **Chito-Ryu Karate** and the ground-truth efficiency of **Gracie Humaita BJJ**.

-   ✓ 4th Dan — Jitsu-Do Karate
-   ✓ BJJ Brown Belt — Gracie Humaita
-   ✓ 35+ years continuous training & instruction
-   ✓ Founder, Jitsu-Do Karate (est. 2009)

“Our duty is not to win — it is not to lose.”
