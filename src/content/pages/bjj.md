# BRAZILIAN JIU-JITSU

GRACIE HUMAITA LINEAGE

## Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — The Ground Truth

Jitsu-Do’s BJJ curriculum is rooted in the **Gracie Humaita lineage** — one of the most technically rigorous BJJ traditions in the world. The Gracie system was built on a simple premise: a smaller, weaker person should be able to control and neutralize a larger, stronger attacker using leverage, timing, and position.

At Jitsu-Do, we take that premise seriously. Every technique we teach has been stress-tested. You will not learn moves that look impressive on video but fail under pressure. You will learn **what works and why** — building the mental model that holds up when it matters.

_“Position before submission. Every time.”_

-   ✓Submissions: chokes, arm locks, leg locks
