## What to Expect at Your Free Trial

We don’t do high-pressure sales. Your free trial class is exactly what it sounds like: come in, train, see if the technical standard and dojo culture fit your goals. If it does, great. If it doesn’t, there’s no awkward conversation.

Every new student starts with a **one-on-one orientation** with Shihan Ginn before joining the class floor. This isn’t a formality — it’s how we make sure you are in the right program, at the right level, from day one.
